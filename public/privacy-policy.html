AskWise Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Summary

AskWise processes your prompt text locally in your browser. We do not operate a backend server, do not sell data, and do not collect analytics or telemetry.

What we access

  • Only the text in the chat composer on supported AI chat sites when you use the extension.
  • Settings and API keys you enter, stored in chrome.storage.local on your device.

What leaves your device

  • Nothing by default. Tier 1 improvements are fully offline.
  • Optional Tier 2: local Ollama on localhost, or cloud APIs only if you provide your own API key.

Data retention

Templates and settings persist locally until you delete them or uninstall the extension. We do not retain copies on any server.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in the AskWise repository.
